\toprule
\# & Assumption & Justification / test \\
\midrule
A1 & The power-management IC (PMIC) regulates the rails, so the phone is a \emph{constant-power} load at the battery terminals. & DC--DC converters hold rail voltages fixed \cite{CH13}; X4 tests constant current. \\
A2 & Component powers add. & Bonn iPhone data: additivity residual 0.15\,\% \cite{BONN26}. \\
A3 & One lumped thermal node for phone and cell; the skin hot spot follows it quasi-statically. & Single-exponential heating and cooling of phones \cite{HE19}. \\
A4 & All electrical power ends up as heat inside the phone (light and RF neglected). & Energy balance \cite{BERN85}. \\
A5 & Sub-second bursts see only the ohmic resistance $R_0$; polarisation is frozen. & Burst $\le 1$\,s $\ll \tau_1\ge 20$\,s. \\
A6 & Traffic bursts are Poisson; radio timers restart at each burst. & LTE/5G state machines \cite{HU12,NA21}; X10 uses an ODE tail. \\
A7 & Battery health is frozen within one discharge (time-scale separation). & Hours vs.\ months. \\
A8 & One Arrhenius factor for the whole pack resistance (cell + protection circuit). & Phone-level $R(-5\,^\circ\mathrm C)/R(25\,^\circ\mathrm C)=2.0$--$3.3$ \cite{LEE20}; X6 splits it. \\
A9 & OCV hysteresis and self-discharge are neglected. & Hysteresis absorbed in $\pm20$\,mV offset; self-discharge 0.1\,\%/month \cite{ZIL19}. \\
A10 & Usage switches between activities as a Markov chain. & Gives closed forms; X15 uses heavy-tailed sessions. \\
A11 & Aging follows square-root laws with Arrhenius and SOC stress factors. & \cite{SCM18,SCH14,KEIL16}; X17 tests $t^{0.75}$. \\
