\section{Assumptions and Notation}\label{sec:assumptions}
\Cref{tab:assumptions} lists the main modeling assumptions and \cref{tab:notation} the notation.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering\small
\caption{Main assumptions. Each one is supported by a measurement, a time-scale argument or a structural
variant (X$n$, \cref{sec:structural}).}
\label{tab:assumptions}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}lXX@{}}
\toprule
\# & Assumption & Justification / test \\
\midrule
A1 & The power-management IC (PMIC) regulates the rails, so the phone is a \emph{constant-power} load on the battery. & DC--DC converters hold rail voltages fixed \cite{CH13}; X4 tests constant current. \\
A2 & Component powers add. & Bonn iPhone data: additivity residual 0.15\,\% \cite{BONN26}. \\
A3 & One lumped thermal node for phone and cell; the skin hot spot follows it quasi-statically. & Single-exponential heating and cooling of phones \cite{HE19}. \\
A4 & All electrical power ends up as heat inside the phone (light and RF neglected). & Energy balance \cite{BERN85}. \\
A5 & Sub-second bursts see only the ohmic resistance $R_0$; polarization is frozen. & Bursts last $\le 1$\,s, whereas $\tau_1=20$--135\,s (default, $-20$ to 40\,$^\circ$C). \\
A6 & Poisson traffic bursts; radio timers restart at each burst. & LTE/5G state machines \cite{HU12,NA21}; X10 uses an ODE tail. \\
A7 & Battery health is frozen within one discharge (time-scale separation). & Hours vs.\ months. \\
A8 & One Arrhenius factor for the whole pack resistance (cell + protection circuit). & Phone-level $R(-5\,^\circ\mathrm C)/R(25\,^\circ\mathrm C)=2.0$--$3.3$ \cite{LEE20}; X6 splits it. \\
A9 & OCV hysteresis and self-discharge are neglected. & Hysteresis absorbed in the $\pm20$\,mV offset $\delta U$; self-discharge 0.1\,\%/month \cite{ZIL19}. \\
A10 & Usage switches between activities as a Markov chain. & Gives closed forms; X15 uses heavy-tailed sessions. \\
A11 & Aging follows square-root laws with Arrhenius and SOC stress factors. & \cite{SCM18,SCH14,KEIL16}; X17 tests $t^{0.75}$. \\
A12 & Constant $\Tamb$ within a run; no battery-saver mode and no charging during a discharge; RP-63 is a generic 2024 phone. & Saver modes only extend TTE (conservative); other phones in V1, V6; other devices in \cref{sec:general}. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}
