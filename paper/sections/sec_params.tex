\section{Parameter Estimation and Data}\label{sec:params}

\paragraph{Reference phone.} All predictions are for \textbf{RP-63}, a generic 2024-class 6.3-inch phone with a
19.5:9 LTPO OLED panel (97.4\,cm$^2$ active area), a 5G modem and one 4.70\,A\,h LiCoO$_2$/graphite pouch cell
charged to 4.45\,V (mean OCV 3.87\,V), as in current large phones \cite{PIX9,MR24}. It models no specific product.
Its nominal energy is 18.2\,W\,h; the usable energy above the 3.40\,V cut-off at vanishing load is
$E_u=17.8$\,W\,h, and the gauge reads 0\,\% at coulombic SOC $\soc_e=0.026$. Other phones (\cref{sec:validation})
use the same parameters with their own capacity and screen size; the pack resistance is scaled as
$R\cdot Q=\text{const}$.

\paragraph{Where the numbers come from.} Each of the 93 parameters carries a source tag (main ones in
\cref{tab:params}). Most
are \emph{published measurements}: component powers \cite{CH13,CH10,GU24,KW19}, radio state machines
\cite{HU12,NA21}, phone thermal constants \cite{HE19,KANG19,PU23} and aging rates \cite{SCH14,KEIL16}. The OCV
curve, $R_1$, $\tau_1$, the low-SOC rise $A_R$ and the activation energies are our \emph{fits} to PyBaMM \cite{SULZER21} runs of the Ai2020 and Marquis2019 parameter sets \cite{AI20,MQ19}; they agree with published Arrhenius
fits \cite{BARC22,SUN23}. \emph{Derived} values are computed from cited numbers, e.g.\ $R_{\mathrm{th}}$ from
measured heating curves \cite{HE19}. \emph{Labeled assumptions} (pack resistance, brown-out margin, burst
multiplier, throttle shape) get wide ranges. Only \emph{one discharge constant is calibrated to phone data}:
$\Delta P_{\mathrm{awake}}$ is set so that the black-screen idle run of the Bonn iPhone 14 Pro \cite{BONN26} lasts
the observed 55.0\,h (exact value 0.0877\,W, used as 0.090\,W; the blind prior 0.10\,W gives $-5.1$\,\%). All
other phone runs are hold-outs (\cref{sec:validation}). The two aging rates $k_H,k_L$ are calibrated to the
manufacturers' statement of SOH 0.80 after 1000 cycles \cite{AP-BATT,GOOG}.

\paragraph{Uncertainty.} Sixty-eight parameters carry an independent uniform, log-uniform or triangular prior
(\cref{tab:params}); the rest are definitions, fitted shape constants, the calibrated aging rates, fixed thresholds
($\Delta T_{\mathrm{thr}}$, $T_{\mathrm{shut}}$) or negligible terms. The nominal (default) set is
\emph{optimistic}: several defaults sit at the low-power end of their ranges ($\Delta P_{\mathrm{awake}}$, $P_{d0}$,
the 5G tail, Wi-Fi active power). We keep them because they are the cited point values (or, for $\Delta P_{\mathrm{awake}}$,
the calibrated one), while the priors are wide and extend mostly towards higher power to cover older or less
efficient hardware. At 25\,$^\circ$C the median TTEs
are 3--38\,\% shorter than the nominal ones, except for throttled gaming (\cref{sec:uq}); for an unknown phone the
median and its 90\,\% interval (\cref{tab:baseline}) are the forecast to quote.

\paragraph{Usage scenarios.}\label{sec:usage} Usage is built from 19 activity primitives with fixed screen,
processor, radio and peripheral settings and a sub-second burst envelope (their powers are in
\cref{fig:power_breakdown}a). Scenarios S1--S8, S16 and CM (camera) run one primitive at a time
(\cref{tab:baseline}). The cold hike S9
alternates GNSS tracking (570\,s) with a 30\,s, 4.1\,W photo burst every 10\,min, starting at 20\,$^\circ$C
indoors. S10a and S10b are the social feed S4 and the scheduled day S12 started cold-soaked ($T_0=\Tamb$),
usually on an aged pack; S11a
and S11c are the social feed and the video call on LTE at weak signal ($-110$\,dBm unless stated). The scheduled
day S12 runs from 07:00: reading 30\,min, navigation 45\,min, standby with a 4\,min social check every 30\,min
until 16:45 (with 30\,min of video and 30\,min of social feed from 11:45), navigation 45\,min, LTE video call
30\,min, gaming 18:00--19:00, video 2\,h, social feed 1\,h, reading 1\,h, standby 8\,h (9.5\,h screen-on). The
random days S13 (typical) and S14 (heavy) are a CTMC
(F6--F8): from idle, sessions start at $\lambda_{\mathrm{on}}=4$\,h$^{-1}$, and activity $j$ is chosen with
probability $p_j$ and lasts an exponential time of mean $d_j$. Typical $p_j$/$d_j$: social 0.55/5\,min, video
0.12/15, game 0.05/20, navigation 0.04/20, video call 0.04/10, camera 0.08/2, audio 0.12/30; heavy: 0.45/8,
0.20/25, 0.15/30, 0.05/25, 0.05/15, 0.05/3, 0.05/30. Wi-Fi and LTE alternate with mean sojourns of 2.5\,h.
