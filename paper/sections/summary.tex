\begin{center}
{\Large\bfseries Power, Voltage, Heat:\\[2pt] A Continuous-Time Model of Smartphone Battery Drain}
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{Problem.} We predict, from physics, how long a smartphone runs on one charge under real use, and
why that time varies so much with the user's activities, the temperature and the battery's age.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Model.} A hierarchy of continuous-time models adds one mechanism per level. L0 is an energy
bucket; L1 a constant-power equivalent circuit, whose current rises as the voltage falls; the main model L2 adds
RC polarisation, a lumped thermal node with Arrhenius resistance, skin-temperature throttling, and a brown-out
threshold for sub-second power bursts; L3 tracks calendar, cycling and lithium-plating aging, which shrink
capacity and raise resistance. Power is the sum of measured component laws (OLED display, processor frequency
scaling, LTE/5G/Wi-Fi radio tails, GNSS, camera, audio). Usage is a daily schedule or a continuous-time Markov
chain, which gives the mean and variance of the time-to-empty (TTE) in closed form. One constant is calibrated;
every other parameter is a cited measurement, an open-data fit or a labelled assumption with a wide prior.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Results} (2024-class phone, 4.70\,A\,h, new, 25\,$^\circ$C, from full charge unless stated):
\begin{itemize}
  \item TTE spans 5.7\,h (3D gaming) to 443\,h (Wi-Fi standby). At room temperature it is the usable energy over
  the mean power (within 2\,\% for steady loads) and is nearly proportional to the starting charge.
  \item \textbf{The radio matters:} video lasts 32.3\,h on Wi-Fi but 21.1\,h on 5G ($+53\,\%$, 90\,\% interval
  $+34$ to $+102\,\%$); a weak signal ($-110$\,dBm) costs 12\,\% on social media and 23\,\% in a video call.
  \item \textbf{Heat caps games:} a game lasts 4.4\,h in a 15\,$^\circ$C room, 5.7\,h at 25\,$^\circ$C and
  10.3\,h at 35\,$^\circ$C, where throttling delivers only 52\,\% of the requested performance.
  \item \textbf{Cold with age strands charge:} on a $-20\,^\circ$C hike a pack at 80\,\% health shuts down in a
  photo burst while still showing 44\,\% (after 12.3\,h, against 27.0\,h warm and new).
  \item \textbf{Usage randomness dominates:} a typical random day lasts $39.6\pm7.1$\,h; the random sequence of
  activities carries 66\,\% of the TTE variance (heavy users 74\,\%), hardware uncertainty the rest.
  \item \textbf{Levers:} skipping one hour of gaming extends a typical day by 37\,\%, the top lever in every
  parameter sample. \textbf{Surprisingly small:} halving background sync ($+0.4\,\%$), sensors (0.6\,\% of the
  day's energy), GNSS on a stationary phone, and battery resistance at 25\,$^\circ$C (under 1\,\%).
  \item \textbf{Uncertainty:} the default parameters are optimistic (median TTEs 3--38\,\% shorter). In the cold
  on an aged pack, resistance and burst size dominate and the TTE's coefficient of variation rises from
  0.13--0.21 to 0.47--0.56.
  \item \textbf{Aging:} charging to 100\,\% overnight reaches 80\,\% health after 2.9 years; an 80\,\% charge limit
  adds 1.9 points of health after two years, and a 35\,$^\circ$C climate removes 12.
  \item \textbf{Other devices:} the same equations and five dimensionless groups carry over to tablets, laptops,
  watches and drones (illustrative parameters); cold, age and low charge push every class towards brown-out.
\end{itemize}

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Validation.} Pre-registered tests on open iPhone discharge traces, LG HG2 cell data, an
electrochemical reference model and published runtimes pass 31 of 59 criteria: idle and camera runs within
$\pm15\,\%$ and the cell model is within 2.4\,\% of the electrochemical model at 25\,$^\circ$C, but the model under-predicts cold capacity loss,
over-predicts stationary GPS power, and Android floors exceed ours.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{For users:} (1) stream and call on Wi-Fi, not on 5G or a weak signal; (2) keep games short, and
cap the screen at 60\,Hz with a dark theme (about $+5\,\%$ each); (3) keep the phone warm in the cold and charge to 80\,\%.
\textbf{For the OS:} (1) a burst governor that caps sub-second peaks at the predicted safe level (at
$-20\,^\circ$C on an aged pack, stranded charge falls from 23 to 2.4\,\% and TTE rises by 25\,\%); (2) a
temperature- and health-aware gauge that moves 0\,\% to the predicted shutdown charge and shows remaining time
with a band (``41\,h, 30--54\,h''); (3) keep light traffic off 5G and batch background wake-ups (5G standby
$+67\,\%$).

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} state of charge; constant-power equivalent circuit; thermal throttling; brown-out;
Markov usage model; Sobol' sensitivity; battery aging.
